# Villa Sinodium — Advertising Plan

**Site:** villasinodium.com
**Property:** Private villa, pool, 3 bedrooms, sleeps 7, ~20 min from Šibenik & Krka National Park, weekly stays.
**Target markets:** Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Austria (Northern/Central European holiday travellers).

---

## Part A — Google Ads (start here)

Google Search is your most direct replacement for the blocked Facebook route, and it's a completely separate system unaffected by the Meta situation. You bid on people who are *actively searching* to rent a Croatian villa — much higher intent than social browsing.

### 1. Account setup checklist

- Create a Google Ads account at ads.google.com (use a business email, not the Facebook one).
- Set up **conversion tracking** before spending: track enquiry-form submits and clicks on your phone/email. Without this you're flying blind.
- Add a **Google Business Profile** (free) — gets the villa on Maps/Search.
- Set language targeting to **English + the local language** of each market, and location targeting to the six countries above (not Croatia).

### 2. Campaign structure

One Search campaign, split into tight ad groups by theme so each ad matches the search:

| Ad group | Example keywords (phrase/exact match) |
|---|---|
| Croatia villa + pool | "villa croatia pool", "croatia villa private pool", "holiday villa croatia pool" |
| Šibenik / Krka area | "villa near šibenik", "holiday house krka national park", "accommodation near krka" |
| Dalmatia rentals | "dalmatia villa rental", "private villa dalmatia", "vacation home dalmatian coast" |
| German market | "ferienhaus kroatien mit pool", "villa kroatien privat pool", "ferienvilla dalmatien" |
| Dutch market | "vakantiehuis kroatië zwembad", "villa kroatië met privézwembad" |
| Nordics (often search in EN) | use the English groups; add "stuga kroatien", "feriehus kroatia" as tests |

Add **negative keywords** day one: *jobs, sale, buy, for sale, cheap, hostel, apartment, camping, hotel jobs*. This stops wasted clicks.

### 3. Ad copy (ready to paste)

**Headlines (mix and match, 30 char max each):**
- Private Villa Near Krka
- Villa with Pool, Sleeps 7
- Dalmatian Countryside Villa
- 20 Min From Šibenik & Krka
- Book Direct — Weekly Stays
- Your Croatia Villa Holiday

**Descriptions (90 char max each):**
- Private 3-bedroom villa with pool in the Dalmatian countryside. Sleeps 7. Book direct.
- Quiet villa near Krka National Park & Šibenik. Weekly stays. Pool, privacy, real Croatia.

**Final URL:** https://villasinodium.com/
Add **sitelinks** (Gallery, Prices, Location, Contact) and **callout extensions** (Private pool, Sleeps 7, Near Krka, Free parking).

### 4. Budget & expectations

Travel/accommodation CPCs run roughly **€0.40–€1.50** per click for these searches (lower for long-tail area terms, higher for "luxury villa croatia"). A realistic test:

- **€10–15/day** (~€300–450/month) gives enough clicks to learn what converts.
- At ~€0.80 avg CPC that's ~12–18 clicks/day. With a decent landing page, expect a handful of enquiries per week.
- Start "Maximise clicks" with a max-CPC cap of ~€1.00 for the first 2–3 weeks, then switch to "Maximise conversions" once tracking has data.
- Watch **search terms report** weekly and add negatives.

### 5. Bonus: Google Vacation Rentals / Hotel free booking links
Google has free booking links for accommodation. Worth exploring once the core campaign runs, or via a channel manager if you list on OTAs.

## Part B — Instagram

**Important reality check:** Instagram *paid ads* run through Meta Ads Manager — the same system tied to your disabled account. **You cannot run paid Instagram ads until either (a) the account is reinstated via the Appeals Centre, or (b) a legitimate co-owner with their own established account sets up advertising.** So Instagram splits into two tracks:

### Track 1 — Organic (do this now, it's free and the account works)

This is where you can act today. In 2026 the algorithm pushes **Reels** to non-followers, so that's how new guests discover you.

**What works now:**
- **Reels, 15–45 seconds.** Short story arc: arrival → pool → sunset → Krka day trip.
- **Sell the destination, not just the house.** Local restaurants, Krka waterfalls, hidden beaches, seasonal events. Becoming a "local expert" gets people to *save* your posts — and saves/shares/DMs are the engagement signals that matter most now (likes barely count).
- **Post 4–5×/week.** Mix Reels, carousels (room tours, "a day at the villa"), and Stories.
- **Hashtags & geotags:** #croatiavilla #dalmatia #sibenik #krkanationalpark #villawithpool #croatiatravel + always tag the location.
- **Bio link → villasinodium.com** with a clear "Book direct" call.
- **Reply to every DM and comment fast** — Instagram rewards it, and DMs are where bookings start.

**4-week starter content calendar:**

| Week | Reels (2/wk) | Carousel (1/wk) | Stories (ongoing) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Villa tour; Pool at golden hour | "5 reasons to stay near Krka" | Behind-the-scenes prep |
| 2 | Krka day trip; Local food spot | Room-by-room tour | Guest POV morning routine |
| 3 | Sunset from terrace; Beach nearby | "What's included" | Q&A sticker: ask me anything |
| 4 | "A day at Villa Sinodium"; Šibenik old town | Seasonal offer / availability | Countdown to summer dates |

### Track 2 — Paid Instagram (ready for when ad access returns)

When you regain ad access (appeal succeeds, or a real co-owner runs it), deploy this:

- **Objective:** Traffic to villasinodium.com (or Engagement to grow the account first).
- **Budget guide:** ~5–10% of monthly rental revenue, scaled to season.
- **Formats to test:** Carousel ads (showcase rooms + pool + views) and Story/Reels ads (promo offers, "summer dates available").
- **Targeting:** the same six countries, ages 28–60, interests: Croatia travel, luxury travel, beach holidays, families/couples. Use **broad + Advantage+** and let Meta optimise — don't over-restrict with tiny custom audiences (that was the issue in the original campaign).
- **Boost your best-performing organic Reel** rather than making ads from scratch — the winners are proven.
