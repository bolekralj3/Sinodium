Privacy notice

This notice describes how the Villa Sinodium website (“we”, “the site”) handles information when you visit. Update your public site URL in this text when you move from GitHub Pages to a custom domain.

Who we are

The site promotes holiday accommodation at Villa Sinodium, Croatia. Contact details are on the Contact section of the main page (replace placeholder contact in the page when operational).

Cookies and marketing technologies

We ask whether you accept optional marketing cookies. If you choose Reject, we do not load the Meta Pixel or similar marketing scripts.

If you choose Accept marketing, Meta’s Pixel may load. That allows Meta (Facebook / Instagram) to receive technical data such as browser information, pages viewed, and events we configure (for example page views and clicks on contact links). Meta uses this for measurement and advertising according to Meta’s policies.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser (for example via localStorage) so we do not ask on every visit unless you clear site data.

Purposes

  • Strictly necessary: site operation (no marketing scripts required for basic viewing).
  • Marketing (optional): understanding how many people visit and use contact or map links, and supporting future paid social campaigns.

Data retention and your rights

Retention of data collected by Meta is governed by Meta. You can adjust ad preferences in your Meta account and use browser controls to block or delete cookies and local storage.

For questions about this property or bookings, use the contact methods on the main site.
